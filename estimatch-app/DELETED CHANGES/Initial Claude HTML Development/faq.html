Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about EstiMatch and how it works with your Xactimate estimates.

General
EstiMatch is an estimate comparison tool built specifically for roofing and restoration contractors. It takes two Xactimate estimate PDFs — yours and the insurance adjuster's — and automatically compares them line by line, highlighting matches, modifications, missing items, and items the adjuster added. This helps you build stronger supplements faster.
EstiMatch is built for roofing and restoration contractors, estimators, and public adjusters who regularly compare their Xactimate estimates against insurance adjuster estimates. If you spend hours highlighting and cross-referencing PDFs, this tool is for you.
Most comparisons complete in under 3 minutes. The time depends on the size of the estimates, but even large multi-zone estimates typically finish in under 5 minutes.
Xactimate Compatibility
We support Xactimate estimate PDFs — the standard PDF export from Xactimate. This is the format most insurance companies and contractors use to share estimates. Simply export your estimate as a PDF from Xactimate and upload it.
Yes. EstiMatch works with Xactimate X1, Xactimate Online, and legacy desktop versions. As long as the estimate is exported as a standard Xactimate PDF with the typical column layout (Description, Qty, Remove, Replace, Tax, O&P, Total), our parser can handle it.
Our AI engine fuzzy-matches items even when zone names and descriptions differ slightly between the two estimates. For example, it will correctly match "Master Bedroom" with "MBR" or "Owner's Suite" and pair similar line items across zones.

Data & Security
Yes. All data is encrypted in transit (TLS/SSL) and at rest. Your uploaded PDFs are stored securely and are only accessible by your account. We use Supabase for data storage and Clerk for authentication — both industry-standard, SOC 2 compliant services.
